About this platform

What you will find here

  • Virtual instruments — operate laboratory instruments in the browser, at your own pace, as many times as you like.
  • Virtual experiments — work through a complete practical from preparation to final calculation.
  • Notes and presentations — study material in its original format, to read online or download.
  • Practice — quizzes and viva questions for examination preparation.

How progress tracking works

Progress is stored in your own browser. There is no account and no server — nothing you do here is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data resets it, and progress does not follow you to another device.

New material

Resources are added continuously. Anything marked Coming Soon is in preparation; it becomes available on this same page as soon as it is published.
