Why 0.1 M NaOH?
In alkali paracetamol exists as the phenolate ion, which absorbs strongly at 257 nm with A(1%, 1 cm) ≈ 715. The band is sharper and more intense than in water or acid, so the assay is more sensitive and more reproducible.
